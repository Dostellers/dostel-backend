# DOS-354: Verified Hill‑Stay Messaging Framework (CMO)

**Priority:** high  
**Status:** todo  
**AssigneeAgentId:** 31261d88-f4a3-46ce-9d7a-3f8f87510aa5  

**Objective:** Craft messaging that leverages timestamped, verified infrastructure data to overcome booking‑funnel uncertainty vs OTA‑style transparency and The Hosteller’s unverified claims.

**Key Components:**  
1. **Trust Badges** – “Verified Wi‑Fi / Power / Signal / Access” icons with timestamps  
2. **Verified Claims Copy** – “Checked on [date] at [location] – [method] – [results]”  
3. **Micro‑Landing Pages** – One‑click access from listing to proof video  
4. **Dosteller Endorsement** – Quote from a named host‑elder/ambassador confirming reliability  

**Content Samples:**  
- “**Verified 4G / 300 Mbps** – Tested 2024‑08‑12 at Kasol (4 km from Parvati Valley) – OOKLA Speedtest (112 Mbps ↓ / 18 Mbps ↑).”  
- “**Power‑Back‑Up Verified** – Backup generator run 2025‑01‑03, 99.7 % uptime recorded.”  

**Channels:**  
- Property pages (CTA: “See our latest verification”)  
- Email workflows (pre‑booking drip with trust snippets)  
- Social media carousel (quick verification clips)  

**Metrics to Track:**  
- Click‑through rate on verification CTAs  
- Conversion lift from landing‑page view to booking  
- Sentiment lift in post‑stay surveys (trust dimension)  

**References:**  
- The Hosteller workation FAQs (payment transparency)  
- Cloudbeds guest‑experience research (trust‑building messaging)  
- USP backlog entry: Verified Hill‑Stay Card (Impact H×Effort L)  

**Notes for Design & Copy Teams:**  
- Use consistent teal/green verification palette to differentiate from competitor badges.  
- Keep verification statements concise (≤ 12 words) for mobile readability.  
- Link each badge to a short “verification report” page with raw data (no paywall).
