# DOS-393: Workation Trust Messaging Campaign

## Priority: High  
## Type: CMO  

### Source  
- The Hosteller workation: 100% pre-payment, no deposit option (web fetch: https://www.thehosteller.com/workations/)  
- Cloudbeds hostel solutions: Standard folio, no community messaging (web fetch: https://www.cloudbeds.com/hostels/)  
- Hostelworld Kodaikanal analysis: Ranking gaps, review volume advantage (competitive-research-summary.md)  

### Problem  
Competitor messaging emphasizes "no deposit" or "all-inclusive" packages. Dostel lacks trust signals for remote workers booking extended stays.  

### Opportunity  
Multi-channel campaign positioning Dostel as **transparent, community-backed hill-stay** with:  
1. **25% UPI Hold** – vs 100% upfront from competitors  
2. **Verified Wi-Fi / Power Proofs** – timestamped Hill-Stay Card  
3. **Dosteller Endorsements** – local host welcome video testimonials  

### Assets Required  
- 3 TikTok/Reels videos (product demo + testimonial montage)  
- 1 Instagram carousel (TH 100% vs Dostel 25% hold)  
- Landing page copy (FAQ section addressing pre-payment fear)  
- Email sequence (abandoned cart reminder × 2)  

### Success Metrics  
- 15K impressions / video baseline  
- 4% CTR to booking widget  
- 20% reduction in pre-payment abandonment rate  

### Distribution Channels  
- Instagram / TikTok (organic + boosted)  
- Hostelworld listing update (verified badge showcase)  
- WhatsApp broadcast list (Dosteller network)  

### Assumptions  
- Creative assets produced in-house by end of Q3  
- 30-day campaign budget approved (target: $500 ad spend)  
- Hostelworld listing update possible within 14 days
