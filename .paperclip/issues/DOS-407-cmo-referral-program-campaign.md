# DOS-407: Referral Program Messaging Campaign (CMO)

## Background
Research in `/paperclip/research/dosteller-referral-patterns.md` shows competitors use gamified tokens (Zostel) and credit incentives (The Hosteller). Dostel can differentiate with a community-driven referral program rewarding Dostellers for bringing new guests.

## Objectives
1. Create a **performance-based referral program** where existing Dostellers earn credit toward future stays or community perks for each successful referral.
2. Launch a **multi-channel campaign** (social, email, in-app) announcing the program.
3. Track referral conversions and adjust incentives based on uptake.

## Key Features
- **Referral Mechanics**
  - Unique referral link/code per Dosteller.
  - Reward triggers when referred guest completes a stay.
  - Rewards: credits toward future bookings, exclusive activity access, or community perks.
- **Incentive Structure**
  - Tiered rewards: 5% credit for first referral, 10% for second, 15% for third.
  - Bonus rewards for high-engagement Dostellers (e.g., top 10% earn extra credit).
- **Tracking & Attribution**
  - Integrate with existing PMS to log referral bookings.
  - Use UTM parameters and unique link tracking for attribution.
- **Messaging**
  - Highlight community benefit: "Grow the Dosteller network, earn rewards."
  - Emphasize ease of use: "Share your link, earn credit automatically."
