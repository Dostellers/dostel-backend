## Objective
Craft messaging that positions Community Event Ticketing as a unique differentiator — guests book stays + experiences in one flow, while local Dostellers earn revenue sharing for hosting events.

## Key Components
1. **Headline Copy** — "Book your stay. Join the vibe. Hosted by Dostellers."
2. **Value Props**
   - One-tap add-on: events appear in booking flow after room selection.
   - Community-curated: workshops, treks, jam sessions, skill-shares — led by verified Dostellers.
   - Revenue share: 50% to Dosteller host, 50% to platform.
   - Vote for next week's events — guests shape the calendar.
3. **Trust Signals**
   - "Verified Dosteller Host" badge on event cards.
   - Past attendee photos & reviews (post-stay).
   - Real-time capacity indicators.
4. **CTAs**
   - "Add to my stay" (in booking funnel).
   - "See this week's events" (property page).
   - "Become a Dosteller host" (for community members).

## Channels
- Property pages (event carousel below room cards).
- Post-booking email/SMS with "Complete your itinerary" link.
- WhatsApp concierge (DOS-504) — "Want to join the sunrise trek tomorrow?"
- Social media carousel (event highlights + host intro).
- Referral incentive: "Bring a friend to an event, both earn Dosteller credits."

## Metrics to Track
- Event attachment rate (% of bookings with ≥1 event).
- Average events per booking.
- Dosteller host earnings (monthly).
- Guest NPS for event-attached stays vs. room-only.
- Revenue share payout accuracy.

## Notes for Design & Copy Teams
- Use warm, community-focused tone ("Join," "Share," "Create").
- Highlight Dosteller host photos/bios — humanize the experience.
- Keep event cards scannable: title, time, price, host badge, 2-line description.
- Color palette: Dostel teal + event-type accent (outdoor=green, creative=orange, wellness=purple).
- Link each event to a "Verified Dosteller Host" profile page.
