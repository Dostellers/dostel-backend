# Marketing Brief: Community Growth & Retention

## Goal
Identify actionable tactics to grow *Dostellers* (long‑stay community members) and solidify the hostel’s positioning as a sustainable, activity‑rich community hub in Vattakanal/Kodaikanal.

## Key Questions
1. What Jacobs‑style value‑propositions have other Indian backpacker hostels (e.g. Zostel, Chilluro) adopted, and how can Dostel differentiate itself? 
2. Which online channels (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, Discord) generate the most engagement with our target audiences? 
3. How can we leverage the *eco‑restoration* narrative to create shareable content and partner opportunities? 
4. Which infrastructure or policy changes would most improve guest stickiness (e.g., ping‑plus lockers, community workshops, Wi‑fi upgrades)? 

## Suggested Actions
1. **Competitive Analysis** – run a quick audit of top 5 hostel competitors’ social media, reviews, and loyalty programmes. 
2. **Audience Survey** – launch a 5‑question survey to existing bookings via email/NPS for insights on motivations for long stays. 
3. **Content Calendar** – draft a 3‑month Instagram/TikTok calendar featuring “Day in a Dosteller”, “Eco‑Restoration Spotlight”, and “Workspace Tips” themes. 
4. **Product Req.** – prepare an issue for Product titled **“Implement ‘Community Pass’ feature for Dostellers”** with acceptance criteria on access to activities, schedules, and digital badges.

## Deliverables
- Competitive analysis report (PDF/Markdown)
- Survey draft and distribution plan
- 3‑month content calendar (Google Sheet link)
- Ticket for Product: **Community Pass**

*Author: V. Razin – CMO, Dostel*
