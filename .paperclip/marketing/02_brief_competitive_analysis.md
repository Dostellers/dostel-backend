# Competitive Analysis Brief: Indian Hostel Landscape (Zostel, The Hosteller, Moustache, GoStops)

## Objective
Map competitor strengths/gaps to inform Dostel's positioning as an ecological-community hostel with long-stay Dostellers.

## Key Findings from Research

| Brand | USP | Gap vs Dostel |
|-------|-----|---------------|
| **Zostel** | Pan-India chain, social events, "Zostelers" community | Less long-stay focus, uniform chain feel |
| **The Hosteller** | Glu app (locks, food, activities), workation packages | Tech-forward but less ecological narrative |
| **Moustache** | Design-led, boutique aesthetic, social spaces | Aesthetic-first; weaker community/ecology story |
| **GoStops** | Budget Gen-Z focus, social-media-ready spaces | Price-driven; limited long-stay/community depth |
| **Airbnb** | Entire homes, "live like local" | No built-in social infrastructure |

## Positioning Gap for Dostel
**Unique angle**: *Ecological restoration narrative* (Bob & Tanya, 1985) + *Dostellers* long-stay network = authentic community + impact tracking.
