# DOS-226 Dosteller Contribution Narrative

## Positioning
**Dostellers is earned belonging for people who stay, contribute and return.**

It is not a paid discount club, loyalty currency or status ladder. Long-stay guests become part of the community by sharing useful skills, helping fellow guests, joining locally led activities and respecting Vattakanal.

## Message hierarchy

### 1. Guest landing page
**Eyebrow:** The long-stay community at Dostel  
**Headline:** Stay long enough to become part of the place.  
**Body:** Dostellers are long-stay community members who contribute what they know, join activities and build a network that continues beyond Vattakanal.  
**Proof points:**
- Share a skill or help host a community activity
- Join locally led walks, restoration work and gatherings
- Build a trusted network of guests, hosts and local guides

**Primary CTA:** Explore Dostellers  
**Secondary CTA:** Book a long stay

### 2. Property-page module
**Headline:** More than a bed in Vattakanal  
**Body:** Stay longer and meet the Dostellers: guests who help shape activities, share knowledge and look out for the community around them. Access grows through participation—not by buying status.  
**CTA:** See how Dostellers works

### 3. Workation pilot
**Headline:** Bring your work. Share what you know.  
**Body:** Work from Vattakanal, then contribute one useful thing: a skill-share, guest guide, activity assist or community task. Dostellers turns a long stay into relationships and local participation—not another workation discount package.  
**CTA:** Join the workation pilot

## Contribution model
Contributions should be concrete, opt-in and verified by a host:
- Lead or assist a skill-share
- Help orient a new guest
- Document a useful local guide with consent
- Support an activity or community meal
- Join an approved ecological-restoration activity

Participation must never replace paid staff or local professional work. Guests should not be pressured to contribute, and benefits must not be promised until Ops confirms them.

## Language guardrails
**Use:** contribute, participate, trusted, share, help, long-stay community, belonging, local guidance.  
**Avoid:** subscribe, elite tier, passive income, token, crypto, earn money, guaranteed reward, free labour.  
**Do not lead with:** percentages, coupons, points or price tables.

## Proof before promotion
Before publishing claims, Product/Ops should define:
- How Dosteller eligibility is earned
- Which contributions qualify and who verifies them
- Which activities/network access are currently available
- Guest consent and moderation standards
- Any financial benefit, separately from contribution recognition

## Competitive rationale
The Hosteller publicly markets paid membership through wallet points and booking discounts. Dostel can differentiate through credible participation and long-stay relationships rather than a competing discount ladder.
