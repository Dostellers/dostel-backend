# Dostel Growth Requirements Brief for Product

## Overview
Based on competitive analysis and market research, this brief documents critical product requirements to fuel Dostel's growth as a Kodaikanal community hostel brand.

## Key Growth Drivers (From Competitive Analysis)

### 1. Integrated Community Platform
- **Gap Identified**: Competitors like The Hosteller use branded apps (Glu) for door locks, food, activities, messaging
- **Dostel Current**: Decentralized (WhatsApp, email)
- **Required**: Unified "Dosteller Hub" with activity calendar, network access, digital badges

### 2. Long-Stay Incentives Engine  
- **Gap Identified**: Static pricing on OTAs; chains offer "Stay 3N, Pay 2N"
- **Current**: Dosteller program exists but not displayed/bookable
- **Required**: Rule-based pricing for ≥7 day discounts, status upgrades, activity bundles

### 3. Experiences Marketplace
- **Gap Identified**: High-demand on-site events not bookable online
- **Current**: Activities mentioned anecdotally only
- **Required**: Online booking for guided treks, eco-workshops, cultural nights

### 4. In-App Communication Layer
- **Gap Identified**: OTA messaging (Glu, Hostelworld) vs. Dostel's external channels
- **Current**: WhatsApp/email dependent
- **Required**: Real-time guest portal for announcements, house rules, peer coordination

### 5. Sustainability Impact Tracking
- **Gap Identified**: Eco-restoration story (Bob & Tanya) not quantified
- **Current**: Branded story exists but no proof points
- **Required**: Impact dashboard (trees planted, waste reduced) linked to rewards
