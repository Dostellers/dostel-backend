Based on July 2026 audit showing:
- Cleanliness score: 6.0/10 (vs. Zostel 10/10, TH 9.4/10)
- Reviews outdated (2020)
- Conversion blocked by poor perceived hygiene

## Immediate Actions (2-day impact)

### 1. Visual Refresh
- **Add 5-7 new photos** showing:
  - Recently cleaned rooms (linens, bathrooms)
  - Well-maintained common areas (kitchen, lounge)
  - Property views (Vattakanal valley, garden)
  - Staff demonstrating cleaning protocols
- **Source**: Use existing property + staff (zero cost)

### 2. Description Rewrite
**Current**: Generic listing missing differentiators  
**New**: Lead with verified strengths:

- Confirm with Ops: Activate "Book on Hostelworld" button
- **Impact**: Eliminates 100% margin loss to OTA commissions vs. cash-only model
- **Messaging**: "Book direct on our site for 25% UPI hold, balance at stay"
