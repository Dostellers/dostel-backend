# Dostel Remote Worker Booking Brief

## Objective
Convert 35% remote worker travelers into direct bookings by offering flexible stay options, workspace‑focused perks, and seamless Dosteller integration.

## Market Insight
- Remote workers prioritize: reliable Wi‑Fi, dedicated workspace, flexible check‑in/out, and work‑friendly amenities
- Current pain points: lack of flexible stay terms, unclear workspace benefits, limited activity options for long‑term remote work

## Competitive Gap
- Competitors (The Hosteller) offer "Workation Packages" but lack Dostel’s unique Dosteller network and ecological narrative
- OTA listings show no flexibility for remote workers; cash‑only model incurs 30% margin loss

## Proposed Solutions

### 1. Flexible Stay Options
- **Remote‑Worker Rate**: 7‑night minimum with 3‑night grace period for early departure (no penalty)
- **Extension Option**: Automatic extension up to +7 days if booked as extension of existing stay
- **Pricing**: Base rate ₹327/night, no extra fees for early checkout within grace period

### 2. Workspace‑Perk Bundle
- **Dedicated Workspace**: Private desk in quiet zone + 24/7 access
- **High‑Speed Wi‑Fi**: Guaranteed 100Mbps+ with priority lane
- **In‑Room Wi‑Fi Boost**: Optional upgrade for $49/day (target corporate remote workers)
- **Marketing Message**: "Work from the mountains. Stay flexible. Earn Dosteller points per night."

### 3. Remote‑Worker Activity Calendar
- **Curated Experiences**: Morning yoga, afternoon skill‑shares, evening networking mixers
- **Dosteller Tier Benefits**: Priority booking for remote‑worker workshops
- **Integration**: Show on `/dosteller` dashboard under "Remote Work Schedule"

### 4. Communication Strategy
- **Pre‑Arrival Email**: "Your Remote Work Package is ready – here’s your flexible stay terms and workspace perks"
- **In‑App Notifications**: Real‑time alerts for workspace availability and activity bookings
- **Peer Coordination**: Board for remote workers to connect on workations (via Guest Communication Hub)

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Remote‑Worker Rate displayed on booking flow with clear terms
- [ ] Grace period extension option visible during checkout
- [ ] Workspace perk details added to product pages and confirmation emails
- [ ] Activity calendar integrated with Dos‑seller Dashboard
- [ ] Metrics tracked: conversion rate, avg. stay length, workspace perk uptake

## Metrics & Goals
- **Conversion**: Increase remote‑worker direct bookings by 20% in 3 months
- **Average Stay**: Extend remote worker stays to 10+ nights (vs. 5.2 current)
- **Utilization**: 40% of remote workers book at least one activity via dashboard
- **Revenue**: ₹8K/month ancillary from workspace upgrades

## Dependencies
- **DOS‑91 (Dosteller Dashboard)**: For activity schedule display
- **DOS‑92 (Pricing Engine)**: To apply Remote‑Worker Rate discounts
- **DOS‑94 (Communication Hub)**: For automated messaging workflows
- **Design**: Update UI elements for workspace perk badges
