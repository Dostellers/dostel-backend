# Social Media Content Calendar Brief

## Objective
Amplify the Referral Program (DOS-96) and grow the Dosteller community through a 30‑day UGC‑focused social campaign that drives awareness, referrals, and eco‑impact storytelling.

## Target Audiences & Platforms
- **Solo travelers & remote workers**: Instagram Reels, TikTok, Twitter/X
- **Backpacker & long‑stay community**: Instagram posts, Stories, Reddit (r/hostel, r/solotravel)
- **Eco‑conscious travelers**: Pinterest boards (eco‑travel, volunteering), Facebook groups

## Content Pillars & Weekly Themes

| Week | Theme | Content Types | Goals |
|------|-------|---------------|-------|
| 1 | **Launch the Referral Program** | - Intro Reel: "Earn ₹10 for every friend you bring" <br>- Carousel: How to share your referral link <br>- Story poll: "Which Dosteller perk do you want?" | Drive initial referral link shares |
| 2 | **UGC: Share Your Dosteller Journey** | - Prompt: Post a photo/story tagging @DostelVatta with #MyDostellerStory <br>- Feature top 3 UGC daily in Stories <br>- Reel compilation of guest experiences | Collect authentic content for repurposing |
| 3 | **Eco‑Impact Spotlight** | - Reel: "How your stay plants trees" (staff + guest participation) <br>- Infographic: Waste segregated this month <br>- Live Q&A with Bob & Tanya legacy team (IG Live) | Tie referrals to sustainability narrative |
| 4 | **Dosteller Rewards & Milestones** | - Countdown to 100th Dosteller (Referral leaderboard) <br>- Testimonial video: "Why I refer friends" <br>- Offer: Double points for referrals this week | Convert engaged users to advocates |

## Posting Cadence
- **Feed posts**: 3x/week (Mon, Wed, Fri) – mix of carousel, single image, UGC repost
- **Stories**: 5x/day – polls, Q&A, swipe‑up to referral link, behind‑the‑scenes
- **Reels/TikTok**: 2x/week (Tue, Thu) – 15‑30 sec, high energy, music‑driven
- **Twitter/X**: 4x/day – short updates, retweets of guest posts, referral reminders
- **Pinterest**: 2x/week – eco‑travel boards, packing guides linking to blog

## Creative Assets Needed
- Referral link graphic: "Your code: ABC123 – Share & earn"
- UGC frame: "Featured Dosteller of the Day"
- Eco‑impact badge: "X trees planted this month – thanks to you!"
- Countdown timer sticker for Stories (100‑Dosteller milestone)

## Hashtag Strategy
- Branded: #MyDostellerStory, #DostellerReferral, #DostelEcoImpact
- Community: #HostelLife, #BackpackerIndia, #RemoteWorkIndia
- Campaign: #ReferAndEarn, #TravelForImpact

## Success Metrics
- **Referral lift**: Increase referral code usage from baseline to 15% of total bookings by day 30
- **UGC volume**: Target 50+ tagged posts/stories with #MyDostellerStory
- **Engagement**: Avg. post engagement rate ≥6% (industry avg 3-4%)
- **Follower growth**: +500 Instagram followers, +300 TikTok followers
- **Traffic**: UTM‑tracked referral link clicks ≥1K/month

## Dependencies & Timeline
- **Week -1**: Creative production (assets, copy approval)
- **Week 0**: Launch day – coordinated push across all channels
- **Weeks 1‑4**: Execution per calendar; community manager monitors & engages
- **Post‑campaign**: Analyse UGC library for future use; feed top performers into DOS-96 referral rewards

## Sources & Inspiration
- Competitor analysis: The Hosteller’s membership perks & referral structure
- Industry benchmarks: Hostelworld social travel trends 2026 (statista)
- Dostel assets: Existing photo library, guest testimonials, eco‑impact data from DOS-95
