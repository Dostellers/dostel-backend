# Dostellers Messaging Hierarchy

**Issue:** DOS-207 · **Audience:** guests, long-stay remote workers, community contributors · **Updated:** 30 Jul 2026

## Positioning

**Dostellers is the long-stay community at Dostel. Members contribute what they know, help shape life at the hostel, and gain access to activities and a trusted network.**

This is not another paid discount club. Belonging is built through participation: sharing a skill, helping a guest, hosting an activity, or adding useful local knowledge.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Lead promise — Stay longer. Become part of the place.**
   Dostel is a Vattakanal community hostel where a longer stay can grow into real participation.
2. **Distinct mechanism — Contribute to earn trust.**
   Verified contributions build a Dosteller profile and may unlock community access or redeemable stay credit.
3. **Proof — Useful actions, recognised by people.**
   Skill shares, local guides, guest help and hosted activities are approved by Dostel staff before recognition.
4. **Outcome — A network that travels with you.**
   Your profile records what you contributed and the community trust you earned.
5. **Commercial support — Benefits follow belonging.**
   Relevant stay or local partner benefits can be offered, but discounts are not the main reason to join.

## Guest landing page

**Eyebrow:** Dostellers — Dostel's long-stay community

**H1:** Stay longer. Become part of the place.

**Body:** Live in Vattakanal, meet the community and contribute something useful. Share a skill, help host an activity or add local knowledge. Verified contributions build your Dosteller profile and open access to the network.

**Primary CTA:** See how Dostellers works

**Secondary CTA:** Explore long stays

**Three proof cards:**

- **Share what you know** — Host a small workshop, language exchange, music session or practical skill share.
- **Help the community** — Support a guest, contribute a useful local guide or help an approved hostel activity run well.
- **Build trusted history** — Dostel verifies contributions so recognition reflects useful participation, not spending.

**Guardrail line:** Benefits vary by approved contribution and availability. No financial value or investment return is promised.

## Property-page module

**Heading:** Don't just visit Vattakanal. Take part in it.

**Body:** Guests staying longer can become Dostellers: community members who share skills, help with approved activities and strengthen local knowledge. Ask the host what contributions are open during your dates.

**CTA:** View Dosteller opportunities

**Compact proof:** Skill shares · local guides · guest help · hosted activities

## Workation pilot

**Program name:** Dosteller Workweek

**Headline:** Bring your work. Share one thing you know.

**Body:** Book a 7, 14 or 28-night stay built for focused work and community life. Alongside verified Wi-Fi and power information, opt into one approved skill share or community contribution during your stay.

**Value order:**

1. Clear long-stay accommodation and work essentials.
2. Optional, staff-approved contribution matched before arrival.
3. Access to relevant Dosteller activities and people.
4. Recognition or redeemable stay credit only after verification.

**CTA:** Join the workation pilot

## Voice and claims

### Use

- contribute, participate, recognised, verified, trusted, access, community
- "may earn redeemable stay credit" only after Product confirms rules
- specific examples with staff approval and guest consent

### Avoid

- token, crypto, invest, trade, cash out, passive income
- "earn belonging" as a literal promise; use it as an internal strategic frame
- invented member counts, partner benefits, savings or contribution values
- suggesting unpaid members replace staff or perform regulated/safety-critical work

## Validation needed before launch

- Product: eligible actions, verification owner, dispute flow, credit expiry/redemption and consent rules.
- Operations: safe contribution categories and staff supervision.
- Community: pilot capacity and first three real opportunities.
- Legal/finance: final terminology for redeemable credit and tax treatment.

## Evidence and assumptions

- The Hosteller publicly leads with paid tiers, booking discounts, cashback and wallet credits: https://www.thehosteller.com/membership/
- Zostel publicly promotes quests, $Zo, maps, events and guest connections: https://www.zostel.com/
- **Assumption:** Contribution verification, portable reputation and redeemable credit are proposed concepts, not confirmed live Dostel features. Copy must remain conditional until implemented.
