# Marketing Brief: Community Growth & Retention Strategy

## Goal
Build on the submitted Dostellers Program issues to create a comprehensive community growth and retention strategy for Dostel.

## Key Insights from Submitted Issues
- **Pricing Engine**: Will enable tiered discounts for Bronze/Silver/Gold members
- **Eligibility Engine**: Will automatically detect Dosteller eligibility
- **Points Engine**: Will create reward loops for retention
- **Community Dashboard**: Will give staff tools to manage members
- **Event Management**: Will enable staff to create activities
- **Display Components**: Will show Dosteller benefits at booking

## Critical Gaps Identified
1. **Retention Loop Optimization**: Current points system may not be engaging enough
2. **Cross-Channel Communication**: Need unified messaging across WhatsApp, email, dashboard
3. **Seasonal Activity Calendar**: Need systematic approach to recurring events
4. **Peer Referral Mechanics**: Current referral program needs strengthening

## Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)
1. **Create Community Retention Calendar**:
   - Map seasonal events around local festivals (Pongal, Diwali, Christmas)
   - Plan quarterly "Dostellers Summit" with village partnerships
   - Schedule monthly skill-share themes

2. **Design Peer Referral Program**:
   - Implement referral link tracking in dashboard
   - Create tiered rewards: Bronze (500 pts), Silver (1000 pts), Gold (2000 pts)
   - Add social sharing capability

3. **Develop Staff Communication Templates**:
   - Welcome messages for new Dostellers
   - Event reminders with RSVP links
   - Retention nudges for inactive members

4. **Launch Monthly Activity Spotlight**:
   - Feature one community event per month
   - Highlight member testimonials
   - Promote special Dosteller-only offerings

## Deliverables
- Community Retention Strategy doc (2 weeks)
- Monthly Event Calendar template (1 week)
- Referral Program implementation plan (1 week)
- Staff Communication Playbook (1 week)
- Metrics dashboard for member activity tracking

## Budget Impact
- Low cost: focuses on process optimization
- Medium cost: event partnerships with local vendors
- High cost: seasonal festival collaborations

## Success Metrics
- 30% increase in Dosteller retention after 6 months
- 25% boost in event participation
- 15% increase in peer referrals
- 20% reduction in community member churn
