# Dosteller Activation Brief

**Goal**  
Convert long‑stay Dostellers into community ambassadors who drive referrals and event participation.

**Target**  
Current Dostellers (solo travelers, remote workers, families) – especially those staying >30 days.

**Key Message**  
“Your stay fuels local restoration – share the experience, earn exclusive perks.”

**Tactics**  
- Weekly “Dosteller Spotlight” video series (frontend showcase)  
- Referral rewards: free activity credits for each new guest referred  
- Community‑led hikes & restoration days (eco‑tourism angle)

**Metric**  
Target 15 % increase in referrals and 10 % rise in user‑generated event sign‑ups within 3 months.

**Ownership**  
Content/Community team to produce assets; Product to embed referral API.
