# Dostellers Messaging Framework

**Author:** Dostel CMO · **Date:** Jul 30, 2026  
**Voice:** Warm, community-first, ecologically conscious  
**Purpose:** Unified messaging for Dostellers digital membership across website, app, and marketing campaigns

---

## Core Brand Pillars (Dostel)
1. **Ecological Restoration** - Inspired by Bob & Tanya (1985), active reforestation and conservation
2. **Authentic Community** - Not just travelers, but a network of Dostellers who belong
3. **Long-Stay Focus** - Designed for guests who stay weeks/months, not just nights

---

## Dosteller Value Proposition
**More than a membership. A community of travelers, remote workers, and mountain-lovers who call Vattakanal a home base.**  
Discounted stays, exclusive events, and a network that travels together.

## Key Messaging by Audience

### Solo Travelers
- **Headline:** "Find your tribe in the mountains"
- **Support:** "Join 3,000+ Dostellers who return year after year for the treks, the bonfires, and the friendships that last beyond the stay"
- **CTA:** "Become a Dosteller →"

### Remote Workers
- **Headline:** "Your mountain office with community built-in"
- **Support:** "Stable WiFi, dedicated workspaces, and a network of professionals who know the balance between productivity and peak-bagging"
- **Perk Highlight:** "Skill shares: teach a workshop, learn something new, all within the Dosteller network"

### Families
- **Headline:** "Where kids make friends and parents reconnect"
- **Support:** "Family-friendly treks, communal meals that feel like home, and the safety of a known community"
- **Perk Highlight:** "Wanderer tier includes free all meals and room upgrades for stress-free extended stays"

### Corporates/Teams
- **Headline:** "Team retreats that actually restore"
- **Support:** "Combine offsite meetings with ecological restoration work and authentic mountain community"
- **Perk Highlight:** "Group booking discounts + volunteer tree-planting activities for team impact"

## Voice & Tone Guidelines
- **Say:** "belong", "community", "network", "home base", "restoration"
- **Avoid:** "points", "level up", "unlock", "rewards" (too gamified)
- **Phrases:** "You know a Dosteller", "Call Vattakanal a home base", "More than a membership"
- **Never:** Sound like a SaaS subscription - this is about human connection
