# Dostellers Program — v2 Design Brief (Community Health Iteration)

## Summary

Adds 10 critical guest‑experience gaps to v1.0’s membership model. These gaps prevent Dostellers from feeling like a real community vs. just another loyalty card.

## Priority Guest‑Experience Issues (to be created in Paperclip)

| DOS | Type | Priority | Title |
|-----|------|----------|-------|
| **DOS‑250** | Backend | P0 | Eligibility & consent engine – detect ≥7‑night stays and capture 3 separate consents |
| **DOS‑251** | Full‑stack | P0 | Long‑stay offer display – show community access perks (no discounts) during booking |
| **DOS‑252** | Frontend | P0 | Staff event management UI – CRUD for weekly community events with seasonal templates |
| **DOS‑253** | Full‑stack | P0 | Guest RSVP & privacy – anonymous RSVP, cancellation, anonymized attendance reports |

## Critical Guest Gaps (v2 Iteration)

1. **First‑Week Onboarding** – 7‑day drip (email + WA) triggered on `membership.createdAt`
2. **Community Health & Anti‑Decay** – Daily/weekly/monthly content calendar posts by community manager
3. **Remote Worker Segment** – Workation packages, desk guarantees, co‑working sessions (profile flag)
4. **Alumni & Re‑Engagement** – 60/90‑day nudges, dormant status, welcome‑back badge
5. **Referral Program** – Double‑sided referral, code generation, payout trigger (fraud checks)
6. **Seasonal House Culture** – Peak/Monsoon/Shoulder event templates
7. **Event Feedback Loop** – 1‑tap rating post‑event, metrics dashboard
8. **Community Code of Conduct** – Digital house rules shown on sign‑up, pinned in WhatsApp
9. **Points Usability** – ₹‑equivalent display on dashboard + checkout
10. **Program Health KPIs** – Monthly scorecard for CMO + Community Lead

*All DOS‑250 to DOS‑253 are P0 items from the community‑experience backlog.*
