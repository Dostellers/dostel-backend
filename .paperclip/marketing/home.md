## Welcome to Dostel

A warm community hostel in the misty forests of Vattakanal, Kodaikanal. 

Built on a story of ecological restoration—Inspired by Bob & Tanya, who came to this hill in 1985 and never left. They planted trees, grew a community, and welcomed travelers with chai and a warm bed. 

For 40 years, we've kept that garden alive. Come stay awhile.
