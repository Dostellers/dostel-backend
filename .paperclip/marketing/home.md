## Welcome to Dostel
A community hostel in the mountains of Vattakanal

---

### The Place
Perched in the misty forests of Kodaikanal at 2,000m. Built on a story of restoration — inspired by Bob & Tanya, who came to Vattakanal in 1985 and never left. They restored the land, grew a community, and planted the seed for what Dostel is today. Forty years later, we're still tending that garden.

This is a place you stay, not just sleep.

---

### Our Spaces
- **Dorms** — from ₹327/night: Where solo becomes social. 4-bed and 6-bed with individual lockers, reading lights, and privacy curtains.
- **Couple Rooms** — from ₹1,299/night: Mountain views with space to connect.
- **Suites** — from ₹1,799/night: Separate living area, kitchenette, and mountain-facing windows. Built for remote workers and long stayers.

---

### Community Life
- **The Dostellers** — Long-stay community members who organize treks to Dolphin's Nose and share stories around the bonfire.
- **Altaf's Cafe** — On property. Parathas at 8am, thali at noon, chai whenever you need it.
- **Events** — Group treks, skill-sharing workshops, movie nights under the stars.

---

*Not a chain. One hostel in one mountain town — run by people who know every trail, every cafe owner, and every firefly spot on the hillside.*
