## FAQ Section

- **"Do I have to be a Dosteller to book?"** — Not at all. Anyone can book a room. Dostellers is for people who stay regularly and want the community experience.
- **"Can I switch tiers?"** — Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.
- **"What's the WhatsApp group like?"** — Travel plans, trek coordination, "who's at Altaf's tonight?" messages, and the occasional dog photo. It's a genuine community, not a marketing broadcast.
- **"Does Dostellers work at other Dostel properties?"** — Dostel is one hostel in Vattakanal. Dostellers benefits apply here. If we expand, reciprocity will follow.
- **"Can I gift a Dostellers membership?"** — Yes. Gift memberships available. Contact us for details.
- **"Can I cancel anytime?"** — Yes. No lock-in. Cancel from your profile. If you rejoin within 6 months, your tenure and tier history carries over.

## Voice notes for this page

- Never say "plan" — say "tier" or "membership"
- Never "subscribe" — say "join"
- The pricing table should feel like an invitation, not a transaction
- Dostellers is lowercase and plural — "a Dosteller", "the Dostellers"
- Avoid gamified language ("level up", "unlock", "rewards") — prefer "access", "belonging", "community"
