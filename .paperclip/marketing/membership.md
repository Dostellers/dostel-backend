## Join the Dosteller Community

At Dostel, your stay becomes part of a growing community of travelers who choose to stay longer and connect deeper. As a long-stay member (Dosteller), you unlock exclusive activities, networking events, and a vibrant community network. From weekly gatherings to skill-sharing workshops, every stay is an invitation to belong.

### What You Gain as a Dosteller
- Access to exclusive community events and activities
- Opportunities to connect with fellow travelers and remote workers
- Priority access to member-only promotions and experiences
- A space where your story becomes part of our collective journey

*Become more than a guest—become a Dosteller.*
