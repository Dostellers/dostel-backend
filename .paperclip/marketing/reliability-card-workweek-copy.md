## Asset 1: "Know before you climb" Reliability Card

### Headline
> Know before you climb

### Subheadline
> Vattakanal is real. The internet, the power, the mist — all of it. Here's what to expect so you can pack right and work confidently.

### Card Body (6 items)

| Icon | Headline | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 📶 | Fiber Wi-Fi | 50 Mbps fiber. Reliable for Zoom, Slack, and code pushes. Not "co-working speed" — just real internet that works. Speed is consistent through the day; peak usage (evenings) may dip slightly but stays usable for calls. |
| ⚡ | Power backup | Generator covers outages. Hot water uses a separate heating system, so even during a full load-shedding window, hot water and Wi-Fi stay on. |
| 🌫️ | Weather | Kodaikanal is cool and misty year-round (10–25°C). Mornings are the best window for clear views. Afternoons can bring rain — raincoat in your bag is non-negotiable, umbrella optional (the mountains ignore umbrellas). |
| 🥾 | Walk to Vattakanal village | 5-minute downhill walk. Auto back to Kodaikanal bus stand is 15 min. No need for a car unless you're heading to the lake or a distant viewpoint. |
| 👤 | Host & community | We're on-site 24/7. Not just reception — people who live here, know every trail, and will point you to the best bonfire spot. Ask us anything at any hour. |
| 📡 | Mobile signal | Jio and Airtel work well in Vattakanal. BSNL is inconsistent. Vodafone is unreliable above 1,800m. Don't count on one carrier — carry both Jio and Airtel SIMs if you rely on mobile data as backup. |

### Measurement definitions (for frontend tooltip / hover)
- **Wi-Fi speed** = measured at 10 AM local ( IST, non-peak ) on the main router. Real-world evening speeds may be lower during peak usage.
- **Power backup** = generator automatic-switch coverage for common areas + WiFi router + hostel kitchen. Private rooms with AC have separate wiring; AC runs on mains with backup for common lighting.
- **Mobile signal** = tested on 4G LTE at the hostel reception point. Signal varies by building/room location and carrier.
