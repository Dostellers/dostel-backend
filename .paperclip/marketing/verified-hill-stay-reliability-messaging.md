# Verified Hill-Stay Reliability Messaging Framework

## Position

**Know before you climb.** Dostel publishes current, method-labelled operating truth for each hill stay, then pairs that proof with a named Dosteller who can help guests arrive and settle in.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Proof:** “Last checked” Wi-Fi, backup power, mobile signal and road/walk access.
2. **Context:** Where and how each check was made, plus honest caveats.
3. **Human layer:** A named on-site Dosteller and the community rhythm guests can expect.
4. **Action:** Book only after reviewing current conditions.

## Approved claims pattern

- **Wi-Fi:** “Last checked [date/time] at [location]: [download/upload/latency]. Tested using [method].”
- **Power:** “Backup covers [systems]. Last drill: [date]. Typical switch time: [range].”
- **Signal:** “[Carrier] tested at [locations] on [date]. Room-level coverage may vary.”
- **Access:** “[Distance/time], [surface/slope/stairs], last reviewed [date]. Ask us before arrival during severe weather.”
- **Weather:** Link to a current forecast; never present climate averages as live conditions.

Until evidence is captured, use **“Awaiting verification”** rather than specific speed, uptime or coverage claims.

## Core copy

**Headline:** Know before you climb

**Subhead:** Current Wi-Fi, power, signal and access checks—shared with timestamps and honest caveats by the people on site.

**CTA:** Check today’s stay conditions

**Trust line:** Proof first. Mountain soul always.

## Differentiation

- The Hosteller lists amenities, transport fares and access warnings, but its reviewed property page does not show dated test evidence.
- Cloudbeds provides digital guest portals and property information; Dostel’s wedge is hostel-specific operating proof plus a human community welcome.
- Zostel’s reviewed homepage emphasizes destinations, trips and app community rather than timestamped property conditions.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim “reliable,” “guaranteed,” “always on,” or exact Mbps without current evidence.
- Display the test timestamp and location beside every numeric claim.
- Mark stale checks visibly and suppress them after the agreed refresh window.
- Separate measured facts from guest anecdotes.
- Name the owner responsible for each refresh.

## Launch surfaces

- Property detail card above room selection
- Workweek landing page
- Booking confirmation / WhatsApp Stay Pass
- OTA listing copy where fields permit
- Social proof carousel using real test captures

## Success measures

- Reliability-card interaction rate
- Property-detail → room-selection conversion
- Pre-booking questions about Wi-Fi/access per 100 sessions
- Workweek cancellation/refund rate caused by expectation mismatch
