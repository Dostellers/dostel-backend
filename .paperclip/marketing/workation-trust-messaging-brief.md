# Workation Trust Messaging Brief

## Goal
Position Dostel’s 25% UPI hold as a community‑investment model that reduces risk and builds trust for remote‑worker bookings.

## Core Message
“Invest in your stay, not just pay for it – earn rewards through verified community spaces.”

## Target Audience
Remote workers (25‑40) seeking trusted, flexible work‑ation spaces in Kodaikanal.

## Key Assets
- **TikTok/Reels (15 s)** – Hook: “Tired of 100% upfront payments? Dostel Invests *Into* Your Stay.” Shows UPI progress bar, badge unlock, and community verification.
- **Instagram Carousel** – Slide 1: Competitor 100% vs. Dostel 25% comparison. Slide 2: “Verified Trust Badges” with investment calculator. Slide 3: Dosteller testimonial. Slide 4: UPI flow with points conversion.
- **Email Sequence** – Subject: “Break free from 100% upfront payments — earn as you explore.” Header: “Your travel budget goes further with Dostel.” Body highlights 25% UPI hold, reward earning, and community access. CTA: “Book with Trust.”
- **Landing Page Copy** – Hero: “Your Stay, Your Investment.” Details UPI hold, reward tiers, and verification process. FAQ addresses trust and flexibility.

## Success Metrics
- 15K+ video views per asset
- 4% CTR to booking page
- 20% reduction in workation booking drop‑off
- 30% increase in verified workspace bookings

## Distribution Channels
Instagram, TikTok, Google Search ads, Hostelworld listing enhancements, Dosteller community newsletter.

## Assumptions
- Creative production can be handled in‑house.
- Dosteller community members are available for testimonials.
- Remote‑worker audience responds to risk‑reduction messaging.

## Success Measurement
Track video views, CTR, booking conversion, and reward redemption rates; A/B test “Risk Reduction” vs. “Community Investment” value props.
