## Workations at Dostel

Work from the mountains. Dostel offers a unique blend of remote work facilities and community living, perfect for digital nomads and corporate teams.

### Why Work at Dostel?
- **High-speed WiFi** and dedicated workspaces
- **Cozy suites** or **shared dorms** for collaboration
- **Community events** to network and unwind
- **Eco-friendly** environment inspired by Bob & Tanya's restoration ethos

### Packages
- **Solo Workation**: Private suite + daily community meetup
- **Team Workation**: Group rooms + shared kitchen + team-building activities
- **Remote Work Retreat**: Dorms + flexible booking + networking events

*Work, connect, and grow in the heart of Kodaikanal.*
