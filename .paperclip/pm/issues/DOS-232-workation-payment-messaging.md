# DOS-232: Workation Payment Messaging

**Priority**: Medium  
**Owner**: CMO  
**Requestor**: Market Researcher  
**Date**: Aug 1, 2026

## Problem
Dostel needs clear messaging to communicate its flexible workation payment advantage over The Hosteller's full upfront requirement.

## Goal
Create marketing copy and assets highlighting "Pay 25% now, finish before arrival—flexible stays for remote workers" to differentiate.

## Requirements
1. **Core Message**: "Pay 25% now, finish before arrival—flexible stays for remote workers."
2. **Landing Page Banner**: Vattakanal workation page banner with flexible payment badge.
3. **Email/SMS Templates**: Balance reminder sequences at T-7, T-3, T-1 days.
3. **Social Media Assets**: Instagram/Twitter cards comparing Dostel 25% hold vs Hosteller 100% upfront.
4. **Tracking Links**: UTM parameters for all campaign assets.
