# DOS-260: OTA Commission Benchmark & Direct Booking Incentive Design

**Priority**: High  
**Owner**: Product Manager  
**Requestor**: Market Researcher  
**Date**: August 1, 2026  

## Problem Statement
Dostel relies on OTA channels for bookings, incurring 12–15% commission fees. Competitors like Zostel and The Hosteller mitigate this through direct booking incentives and loyalty programs.

## Problem Analysis
1. **Commission Impact**: Industry standard commission is 12–15% per booking (Booking.com Partner Programme, Hostelworld Partner Terms). For an average ₹3,000 booking, this equates to ₹375–₹3750 lost per reservation.
2. **Competitive Landscape**:
   - **Zostel**: Uses Zo currency and quests to incentivize direct bookings; offers tiered discounts (5–15%) for direct bookings.
   - **The Hosteller**: Offers membership tiers with up to 12.5% discount for direct bookings.
3. **Dostel's Position**: Currently cash-only payments and no digital loyalty program. Facing 100% OTA dependency for bookings.

## Research Findings
1. **Booking.com Partner Programme**: Commissions range from 12–15% for hostels; higher for premium placements.
2. **Hostelworld Partner Programme**: Offers reduced commission tiers for high-volume properties.
3. **Zostel's Zo Currency**: Earned via stays, reviews, and referrals; used for discounts and exclusive experiences.
4. **The Hosteller's Membership Tiers**: Tier-based discounts (5–12.5%) linked to stay history.

## Recommended Direct Booking Incentive Structure
1. **Tiered Discounts**:
   - 5% discount for 3-night stays
   - 10% discount for stays ≥7 nights
2. **Dosteller Tokens**: Earned through skill-share hosts, redeemable for discounts or exclusive events.
3. **Dynamic Pricing**: Offer discounted rates during off-peak seasons to boost occupancy.
