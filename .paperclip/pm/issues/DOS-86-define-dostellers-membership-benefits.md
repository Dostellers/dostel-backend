# DOS-86: Define Dostellers Digital Membership Benefits as Key Differentiator

**Priority**: High  
**Owner**: CMO/Product Manager  
**Requestor**: Market Researcher  
**Date**: July 29, 2026  

## Problem Statement
Dostel's Dostellers membership program currently exists only as a concept with basic Discord group and no digital perks, according to competitive analysis. This represents a major missed opportunity, as Dostellers are Dostel's key differentiator vs. competitors like Zostel (Zo currency, quests, creator program) and The Hosteller (hyperlocal perks, tokenized rewards).

Without a clearly defined digital membership program with tangible benefits, Dostel cannot:
- Convert long-stay guests into loyal community members
- Create switching costs vs. competitors
- Drive direct bookings through member-only rates
- Build a self-sustaining community that reduces acquisition costs

## Competitive Benchmark Analysis
Based on research of Zostel, The Hosteller, and Dostel's own brand positioning:

### Zostel's Zo Currency Program
- Earn/burn via quests, referrals, social shares
- Creator program (free stays for content)
- Zo Trips access
- Tiered membership (Silver/Gold/Platinum) with progressive discounts (5/10/15%)
- Early access to sales, free night after X stays

### The Hosteller's Approach
- Hyperlocal perks (cafe discounts, skill exchange workshops, laundry discounts)
- Tokenized rewards (USDC or Hosteller token for stays)
- Reputation system (verified badges for helpful guests)
- Tiered membership with progressive benefits

### Dostel's Unique Positioning (Bob & Tanya Inspiration)
- Ecological restoration + community focus
- Local Vattakanal/Kodaikanal community integration
- Skill-sharing and knowledge exchange ethos

## Required Deliverables
CMO/Product Manager must define and document:

### 1. Membership Tiers & Qualification
- Clear tiers (e.g., Explorer → Contributor → Dosteller → Elder)
- Qualification criteria (stays, contributions, tenure)
- Path progression mechanics

### 2. Digital Benefits Framework
**Discounts & Financial**
- Member-only room rates (percentage off public rates)
- Extended stay discounts
- Early bird/late checkout privileges

**Experiential Access**
- Priority booking for popular dates/rooms
- Exclusive access to workshops/skill shares
- Members-only events (talks, film screenings, workshops)
- Early access to new experiences

**Community & Network**
- Access to private Dosteller network/forum
- Member directory with skills/interests
- Ability to host/lead community activities
- Reputation system for helpful contributions

**Local Partnerships** (Hyperlocal Perks)
- Cafe discounts at Vattakanal partner establishments
- Laundry service discounts
- Local tour/guide discounts
- Eco-partnership benefits (refill stations, repair services)

**Tokenized Rewards** (Optional but Differentiating)
- Dostel token/USDC rewards for stays
- Redeemable at partner businesses
- Transferable between members
- Earnable through community contributions

### 3. Technical Requirements for Implementation
- Membership plans schema (tier, price, benefits)
- Dosteller profile extensions (contributions, reputation, tokens)
- Benefit redemption tracking
- Integration with booking engine for member rates
- API endpoints for mobile/web consumption

### 4. Launch & Communication Strategy
- Phased rollout (pilot with existing long-stay guests)
- Clear value proposition messaging
- Onboarding flow for new members
- Regular benefit updates/communications
