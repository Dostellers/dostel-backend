# DOS-95: Community-Driven Hostelworld Listing Overhaul

**Priority**: High  
**Owner**: Marketing Team  
**Requestor**: Market Researcher  
**Date**: July 31, 2026

## Problem
Hostelworld listing (8.9/10) lacks emphasis on community benefits (Dostellers, skill-shares) and shows cleanliness at 6/10. Competitors highlight app features and workation packages.

## Objective
Improve listing appeal to boost organic bookings and differentiate Dostel vs. Zostel/TH.

## Requirements
1. Rewrite copy to include Dostellers community perks.
2. Add 5+ new photos showcasing dorms, kitchen, and community events.
3. Submit 3+ verified guest testimonials.

## Competitive Benchmarks
- Zostel: App-native perks, instant booking, quests.
- The Hosteller: Workation pricing transparency, influencer content.
