# Dostel Competitive Research: Membership & Loyalty Programs (Aug 7, 2026)

## Objective
Analyze Zostel and The Hosteller loyalty/membership programs to inform Dostel's Dostellers digital program.

## Key Findings

### 1. Zostel Membership ($Zo Currency)
- **Structure**: Gamified with Zo points redeemable for discounts.
- **Features**: Quest-based gamification (local experiences, social challenges), mobile app integration.
- **Weakness**: Limited public pricing transparency; points value unclear.
- **Source**: Zostel.com (via app store descriptions).

### 2. The Hosteller Membership ($Ho Points)
- **Plans**: Tiered membership (�₹999/year Roamer, ₹1,999/year Wanderer).
- **Benefits**: 
  - Discounts (5–12.5%), cashback coins (10–20% of stay cost).
  - Lifetime wallet points (no expiry).
  - Exclusive perks: early check-in, voucher access.
- **Weakness**: Benefits tied to loyalty currency; less direct upfront value.
- **Source**: [thehosteller.com/membership](https://www.thehosteller.com/membership/).

### 3. Competitor Gaps for Dostel
- **Dostel**: No active digital membership system.
- **Opportunity**: Leverage ecological mission for unique rewards (e.g., tree-planting credits, eco-activist networking).

## Assumptions
- Publicly listed benefits reflect current offerings.
- Zostel's mobile app features mirror website descriptions.

## Recommendations
1. Launch tiered Dostellers membership with clear monetary value.
2. Integrate gamified eco-actions (points for sustainability activities).
3. Update OTA listings to promote membership benefits.
