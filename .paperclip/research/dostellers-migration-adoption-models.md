# Dostellers Migration-Adoption Models: Research Note

**Focus:** Adoption models for transitioning cash/OTA guests to Dostellers digital community
**Author:** Dostel Community Lead | **Status:** Initial Research

## Background
Competitive analysis shows:
- Zostel uses Zo token loyalty (digital currency)
- The Hosteller offers membership tiers with points system
- Dostellers intentionally avoids points/badges to stay community-first

## Observed Models (vs. Dostellers)

| Competitor | Mechanism | Dostellers Approach |
|------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Zostel** | Zo digital token currency | No monetary incentives, community-only access |
| **The Hosteller** | Membership tiers + points | One simple tier with qualitative benefits |

## Recommended Adoption Funnel

### Phase 1: Awareness & Eligibility
- Display eligibility badge during search/booking
- Show "You might qualify for Dostellers community" banner
- Link to membership benefits page

### Phase 2: Opt-In Process
- Simple two-click WhatsApp group join
- Community code acceptance checkbox
- No data retention penalty for leaving

### Phase 3: Network Effects
- Weekly activity preview
- Guest host introductions
- Skillshare participation opportunity

## Technical Considerations
- Cross-property eligibility calculation (cumulative nights)
- Minimum viable consent capture system
- Automated community eligibility alerts

## Next Steps
Research existing PMS guest data structures to validate eligibility engine requirements.
