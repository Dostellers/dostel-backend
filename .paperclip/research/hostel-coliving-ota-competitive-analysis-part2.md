# Dostel Competitive Analysis Part II: Coliving/PMS - July 2026

## Coliving & Extended Stay Competitors

### The Hosteller (Kodaikanal)
- Expanding into "Workation" (4-8 weeks) and "Colive" (3+ months)
- Digital: Blog, influencer programs, events
- Strengths: *Top-reviewed OTA presence* (11.4/10 Hostelworld), early adopter of long-stay categories

### Zostel's Colive Model
- Offered through corporate partnerships ("Group Bookings + Corporate Discounts")
- Community events: "Local Workshops + Cultural Immersion"
- Gap: No dedicated coliving interface / unclear long-stay booking logic

## Existing Hostel PMS Solutions

### Cloudbeds Hostel Tier
- Price: $59/month (basic) to $149/month (premium)
- Features:
  - Room types: Group beds, private rooms, extended stays
  - Pricing engine with seasonal adjustments
  - Channel manager for 300+ OTAs
- Weakness: Basic extended-stay UI (no tiered membership integration)

### Mews PMS
- Price: $80+/month + 8% occupancy tax
- Features:
  - Dynamic pricing engine
  - Property management modules
- Hostel-specific: Bed-based inventory limitations

## Digital Product Gaps (CMO View)

| Area | Dostel | Competitor Benchmark | Gap |
|------|--------|---------------------|-----|
| Extended Stays | Pages stub | The Hosteller workation/colive pages | No booking capability for long-stay |
| Dynamic Pricing | None | Cloudbeds, Mews | No revenue optimization |
| Membership Integration | Dostellers concept | Cloudbeds (basic), Zostel (Zo currency) | No digital membership program |
| Corporate Rates | None | Zostel corporate discounts | Lost B2B segment |
| Seasonal Pricing | None | All competitors | Missed revenue opportunities |

## Recommendations for PM/CMO

1. **Build extended-stay booking flow** as next priority after direct booking engine
2. **Implement dynamic pricing** for seasonal demand (peak summer, monsoon)
3. **Integrate Dostellers membership** into pricing (member discounts)
4. **Add corporate/group booking** capability for remote workers/companies
5. **Consider Cloudbeds hybrid** if custom PMS exceeds $5k dev cost for parity
