# Dostel Hostelworld Listing Optimization Brief
**Status**: ✅ Complete  
**Owner**: Market Researcher  
**Target Audience**: Marketing Team, CMO  

## Executive Summary  
Dostel's Hostelworld listing (8.9/10) fails to leverage its unique Dostellers community proposition, resulting in suboptimal conversion vs. competitors like Zostel and The Hosteller. Key opportunities include:  
1. Highlighting community perks (skill-shares, events, local discounts)  
2. Addressing cleanliness perception (6/10 rating)  
3. Emphasizing direct booking value (saving ~12.5% OTA commission)  

## Competitive Context  
- **Zostel**: Leads with app-native features (Zo currency, quests) and instant booking.  
- **The Hosteller**: Highlights workation packages and transparent pricing.  
- **Dostel Gap**: Static listing with no community narrative despite strong core identity.  

## Hostelworld Audit Findings  
**Strengths**:  
- Security, atmosphere, staff, location: 10/10  
- Value proposition: ₹327/night (lowest in Kodaikanal)  

**Weaknesses**:  
- Cleanliness: 6/10 (critical improvement area)  
- Lack of community-focused content  
- No direct booking call-to-action  

**Source Data**: [Hostelworld Listing](https://www.hostelworld.com/hostels/p/302851/dostel-vattakanal/)
