## Critical Gaps

1. **Digital Booking**: No direct booking engine = 100% OTA commission (12.5%)
2. **Dostellers Not Digitalized**: Community concept exists but no app/portal for:
   - Activity sign-ups
   - Points/rewards tracking
   - Peer networking
   - Local discount integration
3. **Cleanliness Perception**: 6/10 rating hurting conversion
4. **Missing UPI/Direct Payments**: Cash-only limits conversions

## Competitor Digital Advantages

- **Zostel**: Mobile app with quests, Zo currency, instant booking
- **The Hosteller**: Next.js booking, workation packages (4-8 wk), member portal

## Key Insights for Dostel

- **USP**: Lowest price + unique ecological restoration narrative
- **Vulnerability**: Zostel's community gamification, TH's tech stack
- **Opportunity**: Digitize Dostellers before competitors replicate
