# OTA Listing Audit — Dostel Vattakanal

**Date:** 30 July 2026  
**Scope:** Public Hostelworld listing; other OTA dashboards were not accessed.

## Verified findings

- Overall score is **8.9/10 from 15 reviews**.
- Strongest signals: security, atmosphere, staff, and location are each **10/10**.
- Weakest signal: cleanliness is **6/10**; value and facilities are **8/10**.
- Listing says payment is due on arrival by cash and marks credit cards as not accepted.
- Property copy highlights a shared lounge, garden, kitchen, terrace, evening entertainment, 24-hour reception, Dolphin Nose proximity, and meeting travelers.
- The visible reviews are from 2020, so the social proof shown is old.
- Hostelworld's comparison module currently shows Zostel Kodaikanal at 10 and The Hosteller Kodaikanal at 9.4.
