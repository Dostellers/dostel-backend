## Key Observations

1. **Dostel is competitively priced** at ₹327/night vs Zostel (~₹417) and TH (~₹540).
2. **However**, both Zostel and TH offer added value through membership programs and booking engines that Dostel lacks.
3. **Dostel's cash-only model** reduces conversion — travelers searching on Hostelworld/Booking.com see price but cannot book directly.
4. **No transparent total** — Dostel's site shows starting price but taxes/fees may apply at checkout (Hostelworld shows "Taxes included").

## USP Positioning Implication
- Dostel can position as **"best value"** — lowest nightly rate + community perks.
- Avoid competing on price alone; lead with Dostellers membership value (cafe discounts, skill shares, community access).
- Message: "₹327/night starting. Become a Dosteller. Unlock more."

## Assumptions
- ₹327 is the base dorm rate; suites/couple rooms may be higher (unverified for Dostel specifically).
- Exchange rates are indicative and fluctuate.
- Competitor prices may vary by season, room type, and booking channel.
- "Starting from" prices may not reflect peak-season rates.
