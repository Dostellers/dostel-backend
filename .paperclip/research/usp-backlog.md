## Recommended ship order

1. Choose one authoritative inventory/PMS source; connect atomic bed holds, booking, cancellation and payment. Treat OTA sync and hybrid bed/private blocking as bought/integrated parity.
2. Publish a Vattakanal reliability card with owner, measurement timestamps and clear definitions.
3. Pilot a seven-night Dosteller Workweek using those verified thresholds and a transparent UPI hold where operations support it.
4. Add contribution proposal, staff approval, attendance and the first portable reputation/credit entry.
5. Deliver the no-download Stay Pass, then layer RSVP/waitlist and community-aware package choices onto authoritative inventory.

## Not USPs — required parity or buy/integrate

- Bed-level availability, bed/private-room conflict prevention and atomic holds.
- OTA/channel synchronization, Hostelworld mapping, dynamic rates and multi-property reporting.
- Direct checkout, UPI deposits, payment links, automated reconciliation, chargeback tooling, promo codes, digital check-in and WhatsApp messaging by themselves.
- Generic loyalty discounts, wallets, native apps or AI pricing.
